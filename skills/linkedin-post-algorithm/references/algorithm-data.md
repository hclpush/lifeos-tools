## 1. Engagement weights

| Action | Approx. weight |
|---|---|
| Save / bookmark | 5x |
| Comment > 15 words | 4x |
| Share with commentary | 3–4x |
| Short comment | 2x |
| Like | 1x |

Design implication: a post that makes 10 people save it outperforms one that makes
50 people like it. "Save-worthy" usually means: a framework, a checklist, a resource,
a step-by-step — something the reader will want again later.

## 2. Format reach multipliers

| Format | Reach vs baseline | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Carousel (PDF) | 2.5–3.5x | frameworks, step-by-step, teaching content |
| Native video | 1.2–2x | personal presence, demos, tutorials |
| Text-only | 1x (baseline) | stories, opinions, reflections |
| External link in body | 0.3–0.6x | never — link goes in the first comment |

Practical sequencing (guide's own hindsight lesson): start with text posts, don't
over-invest in carousels early. Earn the carousel effort once text posts show which
topics land.

## 3. Hook patterns — structural skeletons, voice always hers

First line ≤ 9 words (the `linkedin-post` hard rule). The pattern gives the shape;
the words must sound like you. Examples below are calibration aids, not stock.

| Pattern | Shape | Example in her register |
|---|---|---|
| Specific result | did X, got Y | "I taught twelve women Claude Code in six weeks." |
| Myth buster | common belief is wrong | "You don't need a CS degree for AI work." |
| Listicle tease | N things, one underrated | "Five habits from data science I kept. One surprised me." (hook = first sentence) |
| Before / after | then vs now | "Last year I debugged pipelines. Now I teach AI." |
| Curiosity gap | incomplete but honest | "The best prompt technique I know isn't about prompts." |
| Social proof | learned from source | "A student's question changed how I teach agents." |
| Direct address | if you're X, this is for you | "If you left tech burned out, read this." |
| Grounded take | clear opinion, no drama | "Most AI upskilling advice skips the hard part." |

Notes:
- "Controversial take" from the source guide is renamed **grounded take** — she takes
  positions, she doesn't perform outrage.
- Question hooks: only if the next line answers with a punchline (per `linkedin-post`).
- Track which patterns win in her real data and reuse those.

## 4. Storytelling frameworks (post body)

One idea per post. Short sentences. Pick by content type:

| Framework | Flow | Fits her pillars |
|---|---|---|
| PAS | problem → agitate → solution | AI workflow pain points, "how I fixed it" |
| BAB | before → after → bridge | career reinvention, student transformations |
| Mistake-to-lesson | what went wrong → what it taught | build-in-public, teaching honestly |
| AIDA | attention → interest → desire → action | course/offer announcements (sparingly) |
| Contrarian | claim → evidence → reframe | grounded takes on AI hype, upskilling advice |

These map onto the `linkedin-post` 3-paragraph shape: P1 = hook + claim, P2 = the
framework's middle (the substance), P3 = quiet landing.

## 5. Timing & cadence

- **Best slots:** Tuesday–Thursday, 7:30–8:30 AM in the AUDIENCE's timezone. Her
  audience spans Europe + some Asia/US — European morning is the sane default while
  she targets EU consulting/teaching work. Recheck her current location first
  (she moves between countries).
- **Cadence:** 3–5 posts/week compounds much faster than 1–2. Batch-write in one
  sitting (guide suggests Sundays); schedule across the week.
- **Her planning style overrides rigid rhythm:** timely posts (event recaps,
  milestones, resource shares) compress to near dates and can pair on the same day;
  evergreen posts float and reorder freely.

## 6. Golden Hour routine (full)

The first 60–90 minutes determine the bulk of total reach.

- **T−15 min:** comment genuinely on 3–5 posts in the niche before posting.
- **T+0–5:** reply to every comment. Substantive replies (>15 words) count ~4x.
- **T+5–15:** engage on 5–10 other posts in the niche.
- **T+15–60:** keep replying and engaging.
- Total: ~60 min of her time per post. This is the growth engine; it cannot be
  delegated to AI (ToS risk + authenticity risk in the most visible place —
  other people's comment sections).

## 7. CTA options by goal — quiet versions only

One CTA max, and none is a valid choice (default for reflective posts, per
`linkedin-post`'s understated-ending rule).

| Goal | Quiet CTA shape |
|---|---|
| Reach (saves) | "Keeping this checklist here for future me too." / natural save cue |
| Engagement | one genuine question she actually wants answers to |
| Followers | "I write about [pillar] here weekly." — plain, once in a while, not every post |
| Leads | genuine free offer, pro bono framing: "Happy to share the template — ask and I'll send it." |

Never: countdown urgency, "comment KEYWORD" funnels, self-congratulating asks.

## 8. Repurposing matrix

| Source | Target | How |
|---|---|---|
| Text post that performed | Carousel | key points → 8–12 slides |
| Text post | Threads (CN) | reframe for her Chinese-language audience, don't translate literally |
| Carousel | Newsletter/Substack section | expand each slide into a paragraph |
| Teaching session / CCA-F material | Text post | one transferable insight per post — a course module is 5+ posts |
| Q&A or email exchange | Text post | via `linkedin-post` use case 2/3 |

Rule of thumb: only repurpose winners. A post that flopped as text won't be saved
by becoming a carousel.

## 9. Post-mortem checklist (in order of likely impact)

1. **Hook** — would a stranger expand it? ≤ 9 words? Creates a specific expectation?
2. **Golden Hour** — did it happen? A great post with zero early engagement dies.
3. **Link in body?** — instant 0.3–0.6x penalty.
4. **Format fit** — framework content shipped as plain text leaves 2–3x unclaimed.
5. **Slot** — posted Fri evening / weekend / audience's night?
6. **One idea?** — posts carrying two ideas split their engagement.
7. **Sounded like AI/brand?** — low comment quality despite reach often means the
   post informed but didn't invite conversation.

Log findings per post (hook pattern, format, slot, outcome) so patterns accumulate —
the `linkedin-to-notion` tracker is the data source.
